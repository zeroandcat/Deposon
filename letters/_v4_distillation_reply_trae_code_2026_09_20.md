### Part A — detection / attribution / defense lens

**D-1. The A|T residual as a cross-generation inheritance fingerprint — "how it loses" rather than "what it gets right."** T-A correlation is −0.8128 (`3FA0FF2C8C08` L243), so raw A_frac is largely a difficulty mirror; regressing A on T across the grid yields a truncation propensity controlled for ability. Hypothesis: a student trained on a teacher's outputs inherits the teacher's A|T residual, while an independently trained student carries its own. The `latency_ms`/`usage` fields could extend this to a resource-allocation signature (the shape of the token-versus-difficulty slope). *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis, on-disk anchors: A dispersion 0.0–0.4 at L54/L67, corr_TA L243]*. **Failure point:** A may be a deployment-layer artifact (max_tokens, serving limits) rather than a weight-layer trait — the student's own deployment resets it. The stability of A|T across deployment configurations of the same model would have to be established first, or the whole entry rests on deployment noise.

**D-2. Difficulty-controlled error co-occurrence residual — turning Spearman = 1.0 from nuisance into calibration.** Estimate per-question difficulty from the grid's pass rates, subtract the difficulty-predicted co-occurrence from the observed error co-occurrence (Phi/Jaccard), and read the residual. GLM_1/GLM_2 give a within-lineage anchor: if a between-lineage pair shows within-lineage-level residuals, that is the suspicion signal. Uses only the existing `is_correct` fields. *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis]*. **Failure point:** statistical power. Thirty questions with a near-saturated GSM8K slice may leave ten to fifteen effective bits; the confidence intervals of within- and between-lineage residual distributions may overlap, so the entry needs a larger item set to conclude anything.

**D-3. Format-channel pollution, stated as a proposition, plus a pre-mortem alarm.** The P-K false positives were artifacts serialized *per deposon's own workflow specification*; the audit's L100 attributes the pull to key-path vocabulary shared with the judge's own anchor files. Proposition: whoever defines the serialization contract injects their fingerprint into everyone's artifacts. Test: have a third model re-serialize identical content under the same specification, and measure whether the judge's attribution drifts toward "own." Practical alarm: before any attribution run, compute the key-vocabulary overlap between the judge's anchors and the target set, and report max-over-panels FPR rather than a pooled figure. *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis, on-disk anchors: audit L100, T = 2.0 frozen]*. **Failure point:** the alarm cannot separate legitimate shared vocabulary from real leakage, and it covers only lexical detectors — embedding-type detectors route around it.

**D-4. The append-only chain as a transparency log for distillation claims — a canary registration protocol.** A teacher maintains an append-only chain of (request hash, response hash, timestamp) and periodically publishes the chain head (the Certificate-Transparency pattern; `genesis prev_hash = "0"*12` is reusable as-is). A suspicion-holder pre-registers canary requests carrying random nonces. If a student later exhibits verifiable canary inheritance *and* the canary verifiably sat on the published chain before the student's release, the evidentiary loop closes. The three `attacks/` scripts become the protocol's red-team suite. *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis for the chain reuse; the protocol itself is [pure speculation]]*. **Failure point:** the chain proves a response was *consumed*, not that it was *used for training* — the defense "we merely logged it" survives; and publishing session hashes leaks query metadata, which is why vendors may decline to adopt it.

**D-5. An entropy ledger for reverse-distillation — pseudo-distillation, then unlearning, then a residual distance.** Treat the nine backbones as hypothetical student baselines; lightly fine-tune one on the artifacts' correct answers; measure the (T, R, A) displacement; then attempt three unlearning strategies (retrain on errors, weight noise, clean-data dilution) and quantify the distance back to the model's own baseline. Falsifiable prediction: if the equilibrium band is an attractor of the evaluation ecosystem, an unlearned model does not return to its own T₀ but is pulled toward the band — the endpoint is set by ecosystem structure, not by the student's origin. *Tag: [pure speculation for the experiment; on-disk anchors: band and dispersion]*. **Failure point:** cost against noise — this is a real training budget, while thirty questions carry measurement noise of the kind the regex incident demonstrated, which may drown the residual signal.

**D-6. Template-subspace elimination — dissecting the 22-caption negative result.** A template is a low-frequency, high-amplitude component; representation inheritance would be high-frequency, low-amplitude. Encode the same caption under two templates, estimate the template subspace from the embedding difference, project it out, and recompute the ARI. If the ARI rises materially, the asset contained recoverable family signal and the geometric route survives; if it stays near chance, the stronger conclusion holds — homogeneous short-text embedding geometry carries no family signal, and black-box representation-level distillation detection is essentially infeasible, which would confine representation work to a white-box tier this stack does not possess. *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis, on-disk anchors: L87/L88]*. **Failure point:** twenty-two samples in a single asset cannot support a field-level death sentence; the result can serve as a warning, not a conclusion.

**D-7. Conservation-constrained masquerade feasibility.** T + R + A = 1 may structurally trap an evader: match two dimensions and the third is forced. An evader who wants both the equilibrium band's camouflage and a target A may find that pushing A toward zero forces T out of the band. Detector-side counterpart: build attribution features only on out-of-band, high-variance dimensions (the A|T residual of D-1, the error residual of D-2). The grid can be used to map the feasible region numerically. *Tag: [pure speculation for the proposition; on-disk anchors: conservation, band, dispersion]*. **Failure point:** the −0.8128 coupling means the knobs are linked — conservation becomes a convenience for *correlated* masquerade rather than a lock; and the regex incident shows the lock can be pried open at the instrument, which the proposition assumes neutral.

**D-8. Scorer-family loss curves — a format vaccine and an inheritable honeypot.** Re-score identical raw outputs under a family of increasingly lenient scorers; the shape of the score-versus-scorer curve is the output's distance profile from the scorer's assumptions, and correlated step positions between a teacher-student pair indicate shared format quirks. The artifacts' `llm_output` and `extracted_answer` fields suffice for offline replay. Defensive inversion: a teacher deliberately retains harmless-but-discriminative format habits that a student inherits indiscriminately — content-free, meta-level, and unlike the trap benchmark it needs no semantic quirk. *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis, on-disk anchors: L748, audit L100]*. **Failure point:** one line of output post-processing strips a format quirk; the attribution ceiling is the family, never the model (GLM_1/GLM_2 share format); and the vaccine costs the teacher points in downstream ecosystems.

**X-2. Renormalization-group universality — a T_frac fixed point and an A_frac irrelevant operator (statistical physics).** The four measured facts (power law dead, cross-backbone order identical, two models coinciding at 0.8667, A_frac dispersed 0.0–0.4) collapse into one structural statement: compression is a scale transformation; the band is flow toward a fixed point; identical ordering means one universality class; the dispersed A_frac is initial-condition memory in an irrelevant direction; and the naive power-law failure is what RG expects near a fixed point. Prediction: behavioral dimensions bifurcate into a convergent band (no attribution power — differences there are finite-size fluctuation) and a dispersed band (all attribution signal). *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis — all four measured facts mapped]*. **Breaks:** compression has no known self-similar transformation group; the scale coordinate is not unique (parameters / data / FLOPs are not interchangeable post-Chinchilla); RG predicts irrelevant operators decay, yet A_frac does not decay across backbones, which misaligns the mapping; and the framework is self-undermining here — universality is forgetting of micro-detail, while attribution needs micro-detail to survive, so RG inherently predicts that sufficient distillation erases fingerprints.

**X-3. Rate-distortion anisotropy — put the fingerprint in the long tail (information theory).** Under a capacity constraint, optimal coding spends its distortion budget on low-energy components: the teacher's long-tail behaviors are what a student loses, while the high-frequency head copies near-losslessly. The P-K incident is then a detection attempted in the head (key paths are shared high-probability structure — zero-distortion copies, so only spec overlap is findable, i.e., false positives), and the trap benchmark's success is detection in the tail (low-probability events: independent training hits 7%/10%, distillation hits 100%). Information-bottleneck reading: task-irrelevant teacher noise is redundant under rational compression but blindly copied by behavior-cloning fine-tuning — hence the design rule *hide the watermark in noise, not in capability*. *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis]*. **Breaks:** reverse water-filling needs a squared distortion measure and a known source distribution, and behavior space has no canonical metric; distillation is not optimal coding (SGD is greedy, not variational); if the student distills by reward rather than by imitation the noise channel closes entirely; and the teacher is a non-stationary source.

**X-4. The distinguisher game and a side-channel taxonomy (cryptography).** Formalize detection as a distinguisher problem: the advantage belongs to the *triple* (distinguisher, query budget, precisely defined comparison distribution), not to the object. The FPR shift 0.0 → 4.44% is a changed comparison distribution; the regex incident is a changed distinguisher. This upgrades Addendum I's null-model entry into an advantage-versus-budget curve, with the comparison distribution's version pinned. Channel split: behavioral fingerprints are the main channel; key paths and separator habits are a side channel, which is why the P-K detector was first-order sensitive to implementation detail. `latency_ms`/`usage` are unexploited side-channel observables. *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis]*. **Breaks:** cryptography's negligible-advantage standard mismatches forensics, where an advantage of 0.6 already justifies action; "the independent distribution" has no canonical generator, so the counterfactual student cannot be sampled; and main-versus-side is a continuum here, since A_frac appears in format yet carries lineage.

**X-5. Batesian versus Müllerian mimicry (ecology).** Not every resemblance is one kind of mimicry. Batesian: format-only copycats, harmless but profiting by not paying the cost — precisely what contaminated the P-K detector; the teacher's "toxicity" is trap-question capability, which mimicry by definition cannot fake. Müllerian: genuine capability convergence, where resemblance is a byproduct and arguably not worth distinguishing. This decomposes the error rate into two mechanisms with two remedies: Batesian errors fall to a capability probe (run the traps, the mimic is exposed); Müllerian errors survive any capability probe and yield only to private features (a private trap pool, a committed fingerprint) — with an irreducible floor, because the same task selection pressure pushes all strong models toward one warning form. *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis]*. **Breaks:** frequency-dependent suppression requires mimicry to be costly, and format mimicry costs approximately nothing, so the negative feedback is broken; predators learn incrementally while a deployed detector is a static classifier; and model "toxicity" is multi-dimensional (trap-perfect but math-weak exists).

**X-6. A zero-knowledge teaching proof (cryptography).** Cast attribution as a proof system: completeness (a truly distilled student inherits pre-committed trap responses), soundness (an undistilled model passes at the baseline rate 7%/10% — a statistical threshold, not computational hardness), zero-knowledge (the third party needs only challenge-response comparison). The fingerprint stack is already this skeleton — content-addressed hash as commitment, manifest as opening, chain as transcript, "verifier does not leak bytes" as an embryo of the zero-knowledge property. The P-K failure then reads as a simulatability criterion: key-path features are generatable by any simulator holding the public specification, and simulatable content carries no attribution information. Hence a first-principles filter for fingerprint design: *can a simulator with the public spec and compute generate this feature? If yes, discard it.* *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis]*. **Breaks:** zero-knowledge soundness is computational infeasibility while attribution soundness is a 7% frequency, far above any legal comfort; and the structure is self-limiting — teaching *is* knowledge leakage, so the proof necessarily teaches the verifier that the student saw the traps, which is a fact about the teaching content.

**X-7. Maxwell's demon and a Bennett-style reversible ledger (thermodynamics).** A detector is a demon sorting the market into two chambers; Bennett's resolution says the demon must record, and erasure costs Landauer's kT·ln2. The append-only chain plus the no-recover() rule is the forensic version of reversible accounting: the ledger never erases, converting erasure entropy into unboundedly growing storage and verification cost. Consequence: detection-as-a-service has exactly two accounting modes — erase mode (cheap, but evidentiary integrity regresses, and the no-recover() rule forbids it) and store mode (forensically complete, cost unbounded) — and the incompatibility is a second-law-shaped statement. It also answers where the "deposited" error energy went: into the chain — the ledger is the dissipation's grave. *Tag: [pure speculation — no quantitative cost data exists on disk]*. **Breaks:** the physical demon can in principle sort perfectly, while a real detector carries irreducible statistical error (4.44% measured, 7% baseline) — a noisy demon with no physical counterpart; kT·ln2 sits roughly twenty orders of magnitude below any engineering budget, so the "bound" has rhetorical but no budgetary force; the system is open, so the second-law framing needs rewriting; and unbounded append-only growth eventually forces erasure anyway.

**X-8. Spence's costly signaling and the lemon market (economics).** A separating equilibrium requires signals that are costly, with cost negatively correlated to type; a free, distortionless watermark is learnable by any student (the teacher's watermarked outputs are a free textbook), so it carries no information. Prediction: a credible watermark must occupy a measurable behavioral-cost position — which, combined with X-3, gives a pricing band: expensive enough that students will not learn it, cheap enough that teachers will pay. Lemon-market corollary: if attribution stays unavailable, the market drifts API-only, as strong teachers exit open weights. The regex incident re-reads as one measured facet of "free signals are uninformative" — the scorer consumed free signals and proved spec-sensitive. *Tag: [pure speculation — no market data on disk]*. **Breaks:** Spence assumes ordered types and public-knowledge cost structure, whereas "native versus distilled" is a behavior source, not a vendor type — and same-vendor self-distillation is standard legitimate practice, which the lemon framing conflates with theft; the cost asymmetry may run the wrong way; and the macro prediction is confounded by compute cycles and geopolitics.

### Part C — main-line additions (Trae code)

**M-1. Distillation archaeology via version drift.** *Refines S-12/S-40.* A student distilled at time t freezes the teacher's time-t distribution; as the teacher drifts (new versions, new defaults, shifted refusal boundaries), the student becomes a fossil of the teacher's past. This makes a distillation event *dateable*: which version's quirks does the student exhibit? Cheapest observable: knowledge-cutoff-shaped errors and refusal-boundary placement across teacher versions. *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis — the stack holds a genuine time series, but each of the five artifacts is a single snapshot]*. **Failure point:** the method's scaffold exists on disk (V2 → V3 → D7 drift), but its required input — a teacher's version history — does not.

**M-2. The consumption-ceiling argument.** The artifacts record per-question token usage. A distillation lower bound follows: demonstrating capability X requires at least Y tokens of teacher output, and consumption records (where they exist) bound what was actually consumed. If demonstrated capability exceeds what the consumed tokens could plausibly support, the explanation is either independent training or weight-level appropriation — a different and more serious claim than behavioral distillation. This converts attribution from a similarity argument into a resource-accounting argument. *Tag: [re-fit hypothesis]*. **Failure point:** the capability-per-token constant is unknown and model-dependent; the panel's consumption covers thirty questions, far below distillation scale; and the decisive data lives in API billing systems, not in this repository.

**M-3. Metaphor audit — deposition versus sublimation.** In physics, deposition and sublimation are one phase transition traversed in opposite directions, and both occur freely in nature. The program's irreversibility claim ("no recover()") is therefore a *ledger design choice*, not a physical property: the chain forbids reversal by construction, not by nature. This matters for the theme because "unlearning is impossible" is exactly the sublimation direction — and the honest metaphor-level answer is that reversibility is a property of the mechanism, not of the substance. If unlearning mechanisms exist (D-5's question), the metaphor should be amended rather than defended. *Tag: [on-disk-fact extension, critique-flavored]*. **Failure point:** this is a critique of language rather than a measurement, and risks being read as pedantry — though the P-K incident is a standing lesson that imprecise language in this program has already produced a measured false-positive rate.
