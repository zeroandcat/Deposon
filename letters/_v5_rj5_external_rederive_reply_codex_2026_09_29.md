**Q1. The complete transverse-field matrix-element calculation.** Take

\[
H=-aA-bB,\qquad
A=\sum_i\sigma_i^z\sigma_{i+1}^z,\qquad
B=\sum_i\sigma_i^x,
\]

with \(a,b>0\), ordinary Pauli matrices, and \(\hbar=1\). Each Pauli component has eigenvalues \(\pm1\); \(a,b\) have units of energy. Let \(\beta=1/(k_BT)\), where \(T\) is the **quantum system's physical temperature**, and let \(M\) be the number of imaginary-time slices. Then \(\epsilon=\beta/M\) has units of inverse energy. The Lie–Trotter product formula gives, for finite spatial size,

\[
Z=\operatorname{Tr}e^{\beta(aA+bB)}
=\lim_{M\to\infty}\operatorname{Tr}
\left(e^{\epsilon aA}e^{\epsilon bB}\right)^M.
\tag{R1}
\]

At finite \(M\) the right-hand product defines \(Z_M\), an approximation to \(Z\). The local matrix identities that follow are exact for that product. Insert a complete \(\sigma^z\) basis at every slice, with \(s_{i,t}=\pm1\). Since \(A\) is diagonal,

\[
\langle\mathbf s_t|e^{\epsilon aA}|\mathbf s_t\rangle
=\exp\left(\epsilon a\sum_i s_{i,t}s_{i+1,t}\right).
\tag{R2}
\]

The transverse operators on different sites commute, so

\[
e^{\epsilon bB}=\prod_i e^{\epsilon b\sigma_i^x}.
\tag{R3}
\]

For \(x=\epsilon b\), the identity \((\sigma^x)^2=I\) separates the power series into even and odd powers:

\[
e^{x\sigma^x}
=\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{x^{2n}}{(2n)!}I
+\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}\sigma^x
=\begin{pmatrix}\cosh x&\sinh x\\\sinh x&\cosh x\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{R4}
\]

Represent its matrix elements as \(C(x)e^{K_\tau ss'}\). The equal-spin and opposite-spin entries respectively require

\[
Ce^{K_\tau}=\cosh x,\qquad Ce^{-K_\tau}=\sinh x.
\tag{R5}
\]

Their quotient and product determine both unknowns:

\[
e^{2K_\tau}=\coth x,\qquad C^2=\sinh x\cosh x,
\]

\[
\boxed{K_\tau=\tfrac12\log\coth x},\qquad
\boxed{C(x)=\sqrt{\sinh x\cosh x}
=\sqrt{\tfrac12\sinh(2x)}}.
\tag{R6}
\]

Multiplying these entries over all sites and slices yields

\[
Z_M=C(\epsilon b)^{NM}
\sum_{\{s\}}
\exp\left[
K_s\sum_{i,t}s_{i,t}s_{i+1,t}
+K_\tau\sum_{i,t}s_{i,t}s_{i,t+1}
\right],
\quad K_s=\epsilon a.
\tag{R7}
\]

The trace imposes \(s_{i,M+1}=s_{i,1}\); spatial boundaries may be periodic or open, consistently with \(H\). Equation (R7), including \(C^{NM}\), is an exact representation of \(Z_M\). The prefactor is essential for recovering the partition function and free energy. At \(b=0\), take the limit of (R4): off-diagonal entries vanish and equal-spin entries become 1. Separately substituting \(C=0\) and \(K_\tau=\infty\) would obscure this regular matrix limit.
