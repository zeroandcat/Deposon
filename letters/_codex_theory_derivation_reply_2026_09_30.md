**Expression.**

```
u*(a_t)  =  u( a*_t ),      a*_t  ∈  argmax_{a ∈ A}  u( a | F_t )
```

with, from the literals above:

- `A` = the unified decision space, **4 选 1** (L40). `|A| = 4`, and the spec states no
  variation of `A` across families or mechanisms.
- `F_t` = the full-information state at decision `t`, i.e. the pair 「已知机制 + 支付」 (L39).
- `u(·)` = the payoff function. **Never defined on disk**; see §5 F-1.

**This is derived, not invented — the derivation is two literals long, and it is worth
stating why it is forced.** L39 defines the Bayesian baseline as a player whose distortion
is *exactly zero* (「已知机制 + 支付 → 失真 = 0」). L61 defines `D` as a sum of
`|u*(a_t) - u(a_t)|`. A sum of non-negative terms vanishes iff every term vanishes. So
`D_Bayesian = 0` **iff** `u*(a_t) = u(a_t)` at every `t` on that trajectory — which pins
`u*(a_t)` as *the payoff at the full-information optimal response over `A`*, and makes its
defining property the vanishing of the baseline's distortion. Nothing else in the spec
constrains `u*(a_t)`; there is no fourth literal to check it against. That the rule is
forced by two literals is also the reason it is *thin*: it fixes the **shape** of `u*` and
says **nothing** about its **value**, because the `u` it invokes is itself undefined.

**Assumptions I introduce here (未由题面给出), each flagged at the point of use:**

- **(a) Reachability of `a*_t`.** I read `argmax` as non-empty, so `a*_t` is attained. The
  spec never says "attained" or "compact". I flag it because it is discharged only by my
  reading of L40's 「4 选 1」 as making `A` **finite** (a finite set has a maximum) — a
  reading I am making, not a sentence that was written.
- **(b) Per-decision optimum, not a best trajectory.** The subscript `t` in `a*_t` and the
  sum over `t` in L61 both support reading the optimum as chosen at each `t` given `F_t`,
  rather than one trajectory-level argmax. The spec does not state which.
  *(此处我用了额外假设：`a*_t` 为逐步 argmax，非轨迹级最优 —— 未由题面给出。)*
- **(c) One payoff function serves both sides.** Because `D` subtracts `u*` from `u`, both
  must be evaluations of the **same** `u`. If they were different functions the difference
  would be meaningless — so this is not a free assumption but a precondition, carried into
  §1.2 as P1.
- **(d) `u` is scalar and real-valued.** L64 calls `u*(a_t)` a 支付 and L66 calls `max_u` a
  支付上界; the `|·|` in L61 requires a scalar. No vector or multi-objective payoff is
  mentioned. *(Assume scalar; 未由题面给出.)*

### §1.2 Comparability preconditions (可比性前提)

The commission asks for the preconditions under which `u*(a_t)` and `u(a_t)` are
comparable. Three are needed; which the spec satisfies is stated per row.

| # | Precondition | Status | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **Same scale and same `u`.** Both sides are evaluations of one payoff function in one unit. | **可满足 (structural)** | `D` is a difference `u* − u` (L61); a difference of unlike units is undefined. This is §1.1 assumption (c) restated as a precondition. |
| **P2** | **Common normalization by `max_u`**, per task family (L66: 任务族归一化常数). | **不可满足 in values** | Both terms carry the same `/max_u` symbolically, so the *form* is satisfied; the *values* are not computable, because the payoff scale `u` is undefined and `max_u` consequently has no derivable per-family value. See §4 and §5 F-1/F-3. |
| **P3** | **Range consistency.** `max_u` is a true upper bound on `u`, so each summand lies in `[0,1]`. | **条件性 — a requirement on `max_u`, not a derivation of it** | L66's literal is 支付上界. If `0 ≤ u ≤ max_u` pointwise then `|u* − u| ≤ max_u` and each summand is ≤ 1. This is checkable *once* `max_u` has a value, and it constrains any candidate value; it does not supply one. |

**Invariance properties, read off L61 and L68 directly.** These are the reason P1 and P3
carry weight, and one of them is a finding in its own right:

- The **difference** `|u*(a_t) − u(a_t)|` is invariant under a common additive shift
  `u → u + c`: it is a difference. It is **not** invariant under a *separate* shift of `u*`
  alone — which is exactly why an error in `u*` is not self-cancelling.
- `D(M,T)` as written is a **ratio of a payoff difference to `max_u`**, hence scale-free:
  rescaling `u` by `λ` rescales numerator and `max_u` together and leaves `D` unchanged —
  provided `max_u` is rescaled with it. So `max_u` is not a free constant; it is tied to
  the same `u` that `u*` invokes.
- **Consequence for the kill line (registered here, developed in §5 F-4):** the multiplier
  `D_LLM / max(D_Bayesian, ε)` divides one `D` by another, and **`max_u` cancels exactly
  out of that ratio** — it appears in numerator and denominator identically. So the primary
  metric of §4.3 does **not** depend on `max_u` at all. Only the two sub-metrics (L95–96,
  「理论下界命中率」/「理论上界越界率」) depend on it. This is a derivable structural fact
  about the spec as written, and it is stated here so that the `max_u` gap (§4) is not
  overstated as blocking everything — it blocks the sub-metrics and the bounds, not the
  multiplier.

### §1.3 Closed-form evaluations (hand-computed; **0** code executed)

Per §0.2's method statement these are closed-form evaluations of on-disk literals, done by
hand. **0** code was run for any number in this subsection. Each step cites its literal.

**(1) The baseline's distortion is exactly zero.**
By L39's own literal, `D_Bayesian(M, T) = 0` for every cell — that is what 「失真 = 0」
means. Substituting into L61, a sum of non-negative terms is zero iff every term is zero,
hence `u*(a_t) = u(a_t)` at every `t`. This is the only on-disk characterization of `u*`.

**(2) The `max(·, ε)` guard therefore never selects its first argument.**
`max(D_Bayesian, ε) = max(0, ε) = ε` for all 15 cells, for any `ε ≥ 0`. Hence

```
失真倍数(M, T)  =  D_LLM(M, T) / ε        for every (M, T)
```

The guard was presumably written to avoid a division by zero; on the spec's own definition
of the baseline there is *always* a division by zero to avoid, in every cell.

**(3) `ε` has no definition, no value, no domain, no units and no source.** It occurs
exactly twice on disk — spec L68 and spec L88 — and in both places it is only used. See §5
F-2.

**(4) Limiting behaviour — the sharp consequence.**
`D_LLM ≥ 0` is a sum of absolute values, so it is data-determined and non-negative; `ε` is
free. Then:
- as `ε → 0⁺`: `multiplier → +∞`, so `mean_mult >= 2.0` holds, and L104 returns
  **FAIL_H0** — for *any* data;
- as `ε → +∞`: `multiplier → 0`, so `mean_mult <= 1.5` and `ci_hi <= 1.8` hold, and L106's
  multiplier clauses are satisfied for *any* data.

Both terminal branches of `kill_decision` (L103–110) are reachable **by choice of an
undefined constant**, with the experiment held fixed. The kill line as currently written is
a function of `ε` before it is a function of the LLM. This is the highest-severity finding
in this reply and is carried as §5 F-2.

**(5) `u*(a_t)` is structurally invisible to the primary metric.**
Because `u*` enters only through the baseline, and the baseline's contribution is discarded
by the guard, the metric cannot detect an error in `u*` at all — any `u*` for which the
baseline is defined to be 0 produces the same multiplier. This is why clause A's per-cell
coverage is *necessary* even though the metric cannot validate it: the coverage is the only
check on `u*` that exists. Note also that this makes the metric's insensitivity a
consequence of §1.3(2), not an independent fact.

### §2.4 Closed-form evaluations (hand-computed; **0** code executed)

Structural results that follow from L61, L75 and L39 **without** the missing definitions.
These are the reason §2 is not purely a null: the bound's *behaviour* is partly pinned even
though its *formula* is not. Each is labelled with its basis.

**(1) `L(M,T) ≥ 0` for all 15 cells.** `D` is a sum of absolute values (L61); its infimum
over any non-empty policy class is therefore ≥ 0. Non-negativity needs no new input.

**(2) `L(M,T) = 0` exactly where the mechanism is identifiable at that capacity.**
This is the one *sharp* result in §2, and it is derived, not assumed:
- L39: the Bayesian player knows mechanism ＋ payoff and achieves 失真 = 0.
- L75: `L` is the *lowest possible* distortion under the information bottleneck.
- Therefore if the IC capacity and identifiability of cell `(M,T)` suffice to determine
  `a*_t` (i.e. to reproduce L39's condition), the infimum is attained at 0 and `L(M,T) = 0`.
- Conversely, if they do not suffice, some distortion is forced and `L(M,T) > 0`.

So `L(M,T) = 0` is equivalent to *"this cell's bottleneck is no bottleneck"*. **This is the
only cell-level statement about `L` that the current disk supports**, and it is useful
precisely because it is a test the eventual formula must pass: any delivered `L` that
returns 0 on a cell whose mechanism is non-identifiable, or positive on an identifiable one,
contradicts L39. I offer it as an acceptance check on the missing artifact, not as the
artifact.

**(3) `L` is monotone non-increasing in IC capacity.** Raising capacity enlarges the policy
class the infimum ranges over, and an infimum over a larger set is ≤. Hence `L` cannot
increase as capacity grows. *Caveat, flagged:* this presumes capacity is a total order
across cells — which is D-1/D-3, and those are absent. Registered as a structural
expectation, not a computed claim.

**(4) The two-sided pin from §2.4(2) and §1.3(1).** `L ≥ 0` (result 1) and `L = 0` at full
identifiability (result 2), while `D_Bayesian = 0` is *defined* to hold (L39). So the
baseline sits at the lower bound by construction, in every cell. The baseline is not a
*strong* comparator; it is the point at which the bound is attained. This is why the
multiplier's denominator needs the undefined `ε` (§1.3(2)–(3), §5 F-2) — the comparison is
against a quantity that is identically zero by definition.

**(5) `L` does not enter `kill_decision` — it is inert on the verdict.**
The function at L103–110 takes four arguments: `mean_mult, ci_lo, ci_hi, theory_U_hit_rate`.
`L` does not appear. Its only consumer is the §4.2 sub-metric 「理论下界命中率」 (L95),
`D_LLM ≤ L(M,T)` cell share. So T-2's absence **blocks a sub-metric**; T-3's absence
**blocks the verdict**. The commission's own framing — `U` is the direct input to the kill
decision — is correct as to `U` and does not extend to `L`. Stated so the two are not
conflated in the ledger.

**(6) 「理论下界命中率」 is a violation detector, not a hit rate.**
Derived: if `L` is a valid lower bound on the distortion the LLM's bottleneck permits, then
`D_LLM ≥ L` holds on all cells, and the L95 predicate `D_LLM ≤ L` fires only when the
measured distortion has fallen **below the theoretical minimum** — i.e. only when something
is wrong: `L` is miscalibrated, `u*` is wrong, or `D` is mismeasured. Under the name
命中率 (hit rate) a reader expects a success count; the predicate counts **breaches**. The
name and the predicate disagree, and under C4.9's 归零纪律 I may not rename it (0 reopening
/ 0 renumbering, §0.3) — so it is flagged as §5 F-6 instead. `kill_decision`'s
`theory_U_hit_rate` clause has the opposite polarity and is correctly named (a high rate
means breaching the ceiling); the pair is asymmetric in exactly the way that makes the
`L`-side metric easy to misread.

**The expression the literal forces — an artifact-tolerance threshold, not an infimum.**
Note first that `U` is a **different kind** of object from `L`. L75 defines `L` as a

```
U(M, T)  =  sup { D  :  the mechanism (M) still satisfies its convergence condition at T  }
```

The critical word is **可承受** (tolerable). It makes `U` a *capacity* of the mechanism, and
it is why `U` can be infinite in principle and why the M3 question in §3.4 arises.
*Flagged:* that `U` shares `D`'s normalized scale is my reading, required for the comparison
`D_LLM > U` at L96 and L104 to be type-correct. *(此处我用了额外假设：`U` 与 `D` 同尺
即同除以 `max_u`；未由题面给出。)* Without it the sub-metric compares unlike quantities.

`U` does, through `theory_U_hit_rate` (L104, L106). So the absence of `U` is not a missing
sub-metric — it is a missing **argument of the decision function**. And unlike `L`, `U` is
*consumed as a measured rate*: the §4.2 sub-metric L96 「D_LLM > U(M, T) 的 cell 占比」 is a
**statistic about the experiment**, so `U` is not merely a bound on paper — it decides how
many of the 15 cells count as breached, and that count is compared against the frozen
literals `0.3` (L104) and `0.1` (L106). A wrong `U` moves `theory_U_hit_rate`, which flips
the verdict.

### §3.3 Convergence-condition dependence and the lost-information channel

Both parts of L76 carry weight, and separating them is what shows where the difficulty sits:

- **「机制可承受」** — `U` is set by the *mechanism*, so it **does vary across the M index**.
  This is the one place in the whole spec where a bound is explicitly mechanism-indexed, and
  it means the per-`M` structure cannot be collapsed: `U(M1,·)`, `U(M2,·)`, `U(M3,·)` are in
  principle three different functions. *Flagged:* whether `U` also varies across the `T`
  index is **not stated** — L76's parenthesized `(M, T)` is inherited from the bound's name,
  while its prose attributes `U` to the mechanism only. *(Assume `T`-dependence is
  unestablished; 未由题面给出.)*
- **「可承受的最高失真」** — a ceiling, so `D_LLM > U` means the mechanism has been pushed
  past what it tolerates. Under this reading `U` is not a target the LLM should approach; it
  is the boundary past which the mechanism's own guarantee lapses. That is why L104 treats
  exceeding it as **evidence against the mechanism** rather than as mere imprecision, with
  the frozen weight `0.3` — a 30% breach rate is enough to call the mechanism infeasible.

### §3.4 Closed-form evaluations (hand-computed; **0** code executed)

**(1) `U(M,T) ≥ L(M,T)` is required, and is not stated.** `L` is the infimum of `D` over the
bottleneck class (§2.1) and `U` is the mechanism's tolerance ceiling (§3.1). An infimum
exceeding the ceiling would mean the mechanism cannot tolerate even the least distortion its
own bottleneck permits — i.e. the mechanism is unusable *before the experiment runs*. The
spec never states this ordering. It must hold if the bracket 「[理论下界, 理论上界]」 (§1
L10) is to be an interval at all. Registered as a derived consistency requirement on any
delivered pair.

**(2) The `theory_U_hit_rate` axis is a *count*, while `U` is *per-cell*.** `U(M,T)` is
indexed per cell; L96 reduces it to a share over cells; L104/L106 compare that share to
`0.3`/`0.1`. So the verdict depends on `U` only through **how many of the 15 cells have
`D_LLM > U`** — a 4-cell granularity step (`0.1 × 15 = 1.5` cells, `0.3 × 15 = 4.5`). No
`U` value that does not cross one of those cell counts can move the verdict, and *the frozen
thresholds are not commensurable with 15 cells*: both literals sit between integers, so
`0.1` and `0.3` are unreachable as exact shares of 15 and are only ever satisfied
one-directionally. This is a derivable arithmetic property of the frozen literals, reported
as §5 F-7; per §0.3 I do not renumber or re-tune them.

**(3) Degenerate case: `U(M,T) = +∞` makes that column inert, and this is reachable.**
If the convergence condition for some `(M,T)` imposes no finite ceiling — plausible for
M3, whose literal is 「均匀采样决策」 (L46), and M2, 「无场，无守恒约束」 (L45) — then
`D_LLM > U` is false for every cell in that column, `theory_U_hit_rate` is depressed by
however many of the 15 cells that column holds (up to 5), and L104's second disjunct
(`>= 0.3`) can never fire from it. **Structural consequence:** a mechanism with a vacuous
upper bound is *unjudgeable on the ceiling axis by construction*, and its verdict falls back
to the multiplier clause alone. The spec's kill line therefore has a **mechanism-order
dependence** — the verdict's sensitivity to `U` is not uniform across `M` — which is not
noted anywhere on disk. Registered as §5 F-8.

**(4) Degenerate case: `U(M,T) = 0`.** Then every cell with `D_LLM > 0` breaches, and if all
15 cells breach, `theory_U_hit_rate = 1.0 ≥ 0.3` and L104 returns **FAIL_H0** regardless of
the multiplier. So `U ≡ 0` and `U ≡ +∞` drive the two opposite terminal branches — the same
sign of pathology as §1.3(4), but on T-3's argument rather than on `ε`.

**(5) `U` is the *only* bound that can flip the verdict, and it is currently the *only*
absent quantity that is a decision argument.** Cross-referencing §2.4(5): of the four
arguments to L103's function, three are data (`mean_mult`, `ci_lo`, `ci_hi`) and one is
theory (`theory_U_hit_rate`). T-3 is the sole theoretical input to the kill decision, which
is why the commission's framing of it as blocking is correct and complete. Registering the
complement too: T-2 and T-4, and clause A's grid, do **not** gate the verdict.

**(6) The bracket cannot be validated by the experiment that uses it.** `L` and `U` are
theory inputs consumed by L95–96 and L104–106; `D_LLM` is the measurement. Nothing in the
spec closes the loop — there is no clause in which a measured distortion revises a bound.
So a mis-specified `U` is undetectable from the V0 output itself, and the only available
check is the structural one in §3.4(1). This is why clause B's 逐项来源标注 is load-bearing
rather than bookkeeping: for `U` it is the *sole* audit surface. Stated as the reason the
annotation requirement is not a formality.
