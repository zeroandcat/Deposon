## Abstract

The *deposon* is a condensate: a scattering layer over LLM reasoning paths whose every node binding carries two couplings, and whose three scattering channels — transmitted, reflected, and dissipated — sum to identity by construction. The published first paper established the mechanism and its conservation ledger. This paper reports what happened next, as one continuous arc. The question the arc pursues is the one the first paper's value proposition leaves open: the condensate does not promise to be more accurate — it promises to *keep the books*, and to keep them recomputable, step by step, by any third party. What, then, is such a ledger worth, and what does its trust actually rest on? The arc descends through six faces of that question. On the constructive face, the strongest reading of the ledger — the potential-game reading — survives four construction-face legs while its central universal proposition is killed at claim level, coverage 4/22 graphs. On the robustness face, the ledger's readings fail their immunity, recount, and degeneration re-examinations, with root causes registered not as real falsifications but as fake falsifications and uncomputability — propositions untouched, constructions unsolved. On the live face, the layer's meeting with real LLM players is registered (dual calibers 80.0% / 73.3%, co-reported) while the distortion-bound experiment that would formalize the layer's central question stands blocked, its forward payoff values adopted as design inputs (2026-10-12) and its theoretical bounds still undelivered. On the adjudicative face, the arc turns on itself: verdicts are shown to require root-cause attribution before they mean anything — an instrument self-audit recovers a falsified verdict, the same literal PASS carries three different root-cause tiers, and a teacher-metadata probe triggers a real falsification. On the transfer face, the attempt to teach the governing judgment methods to an AI by critical learning fails across three pre-registered rounds (structural similarity 0.0909; critique coverage 0.6842; blind obedience 0.3333; five of six kill-lines in the final round), root causes shifting from construction-dominated to compound. On the substrate face, the judgment ledger's evidence base — an eight-wave, 180-entry collection governed by the same pre-registration discipline — passes its collection-face criteria on two legs and closes known-defects on a third, while a supplementary fourth line fires at its hit line (twelve readings, none reaching the coverage line; closed today on a local chain, the main verdict untouched), with a shared substrate gate closed but not released. The arc ends where it began: both ledgers, machine and human, keep their books under one discipline, and nothing here is a claim stronger than the literal wording of the data.

**Keywords:** auditability; conservation identity; physics-inspired formalism; pre-registered kill-lines; negative results; chain-of-thought; critical learning; distillation

## 1 Introduction

### 1.1 The gap the condensate was built for

Multi-step LLM reasoning currently has two auditable shapes. *Post-hoc provenance* audits a run after the fact, from artifacts the run leaves behind; it can inspect both sides of a run but requires after-the-fact forensics. *Cryptographic proofs* audit inside the run; they are narrow and expensive. Between the two sits an empty layer that neither covers: **runtime, per-instance invariants** — invariants that hold inside the run, at per-instance granularity, without extra cost, and without presupposing an attack model. The first paper of this program [R0] proposed a construction for exactly this layer: the *deposon*, a condensate scattering layer bound to LLM reasoning paths, auditable and conservation-guaranteed, with its game-theoretic face already tested. That paper's compressed value proposition is the seed of everything reported here: the condensate is **not more accurate, but it keeps the books — and the books can be recomputed step by step by any third party with double-precision arithmetic**.

### 1.2 One arc, not two tracks

This paper is the continuation of the program, and its repository self-declares the trajectory it continues: two completed phases, already published together as the first paper (the mind-map phase and the game-theory-explanation phase), and an ongoing research span whose record this paper is. That span covers two bodies of work — the adversarial validation of the machine ledger, and the critical-learning transfer of the judgment methods that govern the audit — but this paper reports them as **one arc**, because that is what they turn out to be. The machine ledger's validation ends by putting the *judge* on the table: what a reading means is a judgment, and the judgment has its own ledger, collected wave by wave under the same pre-registration discipline, audited by the same recompute rule. The arc of this paper is the full length of that chain: from the books, to the readings, to the verdicts, to the judgments, to the evidence base beneath the judgments, and back to the books.

The arc has six faces, and the faces are the paper's structure. §4.1 asks what survives the strongest reading of the ledger (the constructive face). §4.2 asks whether the ledger's readings hold up under confound control, recount, and degeneration pressure (the robustness face). §4.3 asks what happens when the layer meets real LLM players, and formalizes the central distortion question that remains blocked (the live face). §4.4 asks whether the verdicts themselves can be trusted — an instrument audits itself, a literal PASS is shown to carry three different causes, and a teacher-metadata probe triggers a real falsification (the adjudicative face). §4.5 asks whether the judgment methods that govern this whole discipline can be taught to an AI by critical learning — three pre-registered rounds say no, within their construction domains (the transfer face). §4.6 asks what the judgment's own evidence base is worth, and closes the arc where it opened: the judgment ledger and the machine ledger share one substrate and one discipline (the substrate face).

### 1.3 What this paper claims, and how it claims it

The claims are organized as a tree with one root. The root is the condensate's value proposition (§2). Under it, six face-claims (A1–A6, one per face of §4) each carry their own verdict literals; the tree is stated in full in §5, and every node is checkable against a sealed on-repo artifact by path, sha256[:12], and byte count (Appendix A). The discipline that makes the claims checkable — pre-registered kill-lines, three-state verdicts, root-cause classification, bidirectional extrapolation bans — is the paper's *instrument*, not its thesis (§3). Negative results are reported as part of the evidence, at the same weight as closed conclusions; the register of outcomes (§5) is not an apology but a map.

## 2 The Condensate: One Ontology, One Ledger

This section is the trunk the whole arc grows from. It is stated once, compactly, in six layers, following the first paper's own order.

### 2.1 Representation: concept-decomposition graphs

An LLM backend decomposes a task into a directed concept graph G=(V,E) whose nodes are of five kinds — numeric, operation, trap/decoy, answer-candidate, and general-concept nodes. Candidate paths are generated by BFS and ranked shortest-first. The corpus face is published and frozen: 24 files under `corpus/v20/` (16 structural-family graphs, 6 real-semantic-family graphs, index and README), with the index carrying its own fingerprint (Appendix E). The 22-graph judgment corpus used in §4.1 is a subset of this face; four of its graphs are material to the arc's first face.

### 2.2 Mechanism: the Deposon state and three-channel scattering

Every node binds two couplings — a path coupling g_couple and an aether coupling g_aether (resonance energy E₀) — and every reasoning path is scattered through three channels:

- **T (transmission)** — the path's retained share;
- **R (reflection)** — the path's rejected / rolled-back share;
- **A (dissipation)** — the path's irreversible deposit into the aether: non-volatile bookkeeping with **no recovery channel**.

The scattering formula holds for arbitrary parameters: Λ = 1 + g_eff + g_aether, with T = 1/Λ, R = g_eff/Λ, A = g_aether/Λ, and g_eff = g_couple/(1+δ²) (a Lorentzian detuning factor). Two analytic limit states bracket the construction: the blocking state (g_aether → 0) and the tunneling state (g_aether ≫ 0), continuously interpolated by η = g_aether/g_couple. One honesty lock from the first paper travels with the mechanism and is restated here verbatim in force: the scattering form is *inspired by* Feshbach resonance; the T/R/A weights are **constructive definitions, not S-matrix derivations**. The physics vocabulary is a fixed borrow, not a physical derivation.

### 2.3 Conservation: the ledger

The core invariant is **T + R + A ≡ 1**, constructive for arbitrary parameters. The per-path energy ledger closes by one-step induction: E⁽ⁿ⁾ + E_refl + E_diss = E⁽⁰⁾. The first paper's flagship reading, quoted as published: **maximum per-path energy-audit deviation 2.2e-16 (machine precision)** — rejected paths leave a machine-recomputable record instead of disappearing. Irreversibility is implemented at source level — `dissipate()` accumulates monotonically into a persistent counter, and there is no `recover()` — and argued theoretically: dissipation into an infinite-dimensional orthogonal state space defeats Poincaré recurrence. The first paper's own honest boundary travels with the claim: any finite-dimensional implementation has a Poincaré recurrence time (numerically many orders beyond the age of the universe); theoretical irreversibility and implementational finitude are co-reported.

### 2.4 Dynamics: reverse dynamics and the potential-game face

## 3 The Instrument: Pre-Registered Kill-Lines as Governance

The instrument of the arc is a governance layer, stated here once and applied everywhere. Its positioning is a priori and narrow: **the kill-line discipline is a setting of the instrument, not a result.** Three sentences fix its epistemic status. First, a pre-registered criterion specifies the *falsification conditions* of a claim — it does not specify the claim's content; the content comes from the construction and the readings. Second, every claim of this paper can be examined independently of the criteria; the criteria only decide which claims go out. On the surviving side, the criteria decide nothing further: the relative ordering of surviving claims is carried by the readings, not by the verdicts, and stands outside the instrument. Third, an a-priori setting is not by itself evidence — which is why every closed verdict discloses, side by side, its criterion literal, its freeze timestamp, and its recompute entry. The a-priori character also bounds what the criteria can register: a criterion registers only the failure modes foreseeable at the moment it is frozen; failure modes outside the registered family are caught — if they are caught — by the root-cause classification, not by the criterion.

The instrument's twelve rules, compressed to their operational content: (1) three-state verdicts only — PASS, FAIL, or KD (known defects), no graded language; (2) freeze before execution, effective-locked, no post-freeze amendment, `new_thresholds_added = 0` verified per verdict; (3) verdicts are pure functions of pre-registered criteria; (4) readings are transcribed at face value, connectives banned from verdict statements; (5) every FAIL carries a root cause in a three-way classification — real falsification, fake falsification (the experiment failed, not the proposition), or mixed; (6) bidirectional extrapolation bans at every FAIL and every construction-face PASS — a fake falsification does not kill the proposition, a construction-face PASS does not license a real-face claim; (7) registered divergences are co-reported, never resolved in text; (8) hung items stay hung ("not found → pending PI" is the accepted form); (9) nothing in the paper takes effect by itself — adoption is the PI's action; (10) counting discipline — counts quoted with time points, no stale counts as current values; (11) the verdict vocabulary is time-layered — this paper's judgment faces are all of an earlier date and use the original three-state vocabulary, while a later five-terminal vocabulary is registered as protocol evolution only; (12) negative results are part of the evidence, at the same weight as closed conclusions. One standing risk is registered alongside the rules: when a criterion becomes an execution target, runs drift toward satisfying the criterion rather than the claim it guards; the transcription rules (readings at face value, connectives banned from verdict statements) suppress that drift, and do not eliminate it.

The instrument connects to the first paper's published three-tier evidence scale (closed / consistency / motivation only) by mapping: closed (pre-registered) ↔ the three-state verdict face; consistency ↔ consistency evidence; motivation only ↔ motivation. The narrative follows the published tier vocabulary; the three-state machinery is governance infrastructure, stated in this section and in Appendix B, and is not itself a claim of this paper.

### 4.1 The constructive face: what survives the strongest reading

The strongest surviving reading of the ledger is the potential-game quartet of §2.4. **The root cause.** The claim verdict's root cause is registered as **mixed**, in two layers, transcribed. At the construction-face layer, the B-leg failure is a real falsification — the root-cause triad's class ①: the reading and tool faces show no failure (the Φ definition, tolerance, and endpoint machinery all recompute), so the violation is in the material face. At the proposition-face layer, the elements for a full real-falsification call (③④) are not satisfied — coverage 18.2% against a universal proposition, with two of three violations at negligible magnitude — hence the composite: **class ① at the construction face, ③④ unmet at the proposition face ⇒ ③ mixed.** The distinction between the triad's class ① and the four-element real-falsification test is registered, not conflated.

**The boundary.** The claim-level FAIL licenses no statement that the potential-game reading is dead: 18 of 22 graphs were never judged, and the pre-registration itself bans the extrapolation. Symmetrically, the five construction-face PASS legs license no real-face claim: they are construction-face closures, and Rule 6 cuts both ways. What survives, exactly, is a four-legged construction-face reading with a killed universal proposition at 18.2% coverage — the arc's first settled face.

**Bridge.** A reading that survives at construction face must next survive being *attacked* — by confounds, by recounts, and by degeneration. The robustness face is where the ledger's readings themselves are put under load.

### 4.2 The robustness face: immunity, recount, and the protection of readings
