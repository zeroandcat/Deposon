## 3. Abstract（摘要，表单变体，实测 1,906 字符 ≤ 1,920）

```
The *deposon* is a condensate: a scattering layer over LLM reasoning paths whose three channels sum to identity by construction. The first paper established the mechanism and conservation ledger; this paper reports the audit that followed, as one arc. It does not promise accuracy — it promises to keep the books, recomputable by any third party; the arc asks what that trust rests on, through six faces. On the constructive face, the potential-game reading survives four construction-face legs while its universal proposition is killed at claim level, coverage 4/22 graphs. On the robustness face, the readings fail immunity, recount, and degeneration re-examinations; root causes register as fake falsification and uncomputability — propositions untouched, constructions unsolved. On the live face, real LLM players are met under dual calibers (80.0% / 73.3%, co-reported); the distortion-bound experiment is blocked on a missing theory input. On the adjudicative face, verdicts require root-cause attribution: a self-audit recovers a falsified verdict, one literal PASS carries three root-cause tiers, a teacher-metadata probe triggers a real falsification. On the transfer face, teaching its judgment methods to an AI by critical learning fails across three pre-registered rounds (structural similarity 0.0909; critique coverage 0.6842; blind obedience 0.3333; 5/6 kill-lines in the final round); root causes shift from construction-dominated to compound. On the substrate face, the judgment ledger's substrate — eight waves, 180 entries under the same discipline — passes two collection legs, closes known-defects on a third, fires a supplementary fourth line at its hit line (twelve readings, none ≥ 1.00); the shared substrate gate is closed, not released. Both ledgers, machine and human, keep their books under one discipline; nothing here is a claim stronger than the literal wording of the data.
```
