## ① C-3：[R37]/[R36] 四判据核验

**0. 稿件原文（实测 L176；M1–M5 挂点 L186–L190）**：
> "The genre's reference entry defines hard SF by its demand for internal consistency — the world's books must run no deficit — and by the requirement that the world's claims be checkable by observers inside the world [R37]. The anthology statement that frames the modern hard-SF canon adds two more: the story writes no supernatural checks, and its world stays continuous with known science [R36]. ... the mapping below inherits exactly these four demands and nothing else."

**1. 短语级检索：四句转述在素材池内零命中。** 对全池 69 件 grep `no deficit|checkable by observers|books must|internal consistency|supernatural checks|continuous with known science`：仅 q03.json（泛网检索快照：Facebook/writing.SE/Jemisin 博客/storyloft 等非一手页）含 "internal consistency" 泛用例；四个特征短语在 sfe_hard_sf.txt、wiki_hardsf.txt、cramer_tf.txt 及全部其余文件中**0 命中**。

**2. [R37] SFE 词条实文**（sfe_hard_sf.txt/json，URL= sf-encyclopedia.com/entry/hard_sf，词条头："Hard SF. **Entry updated 7 April 2025**. Tagged: Theme"，署名 [PN/JC]；术语由 P. Schuyler Miller 于 Astounding 1957 年 11 月始用）：
- **Q1**（词条引 Allen Steele, "Hard Again", New York Review of Science Fiction, June 1992）："Hard sf is the form of imaginative literature that uses either established or carefully extrapolated science as its backbone."
- **Q2**："Hard sf should not, however, wilfully ignore or break known scientific principles"
- **Q3**："While a rigorous definition of 'hard sf' may be impossible, perhaps the most important thing about it is, not that it should include real science in any great detail, but that it should respect the scientific spirit; it should seek to provide natural rather than supernatural or transcendental explanations for the events and phenomena it describes."
- 词条**不含** "internal consistency"（该词组 0 命中），亦无 "books run no deficit"、"checkable by observers" 类表述；且明言"rigorous definition may be impossible"。

**4. 判定**：

## ② C-4：Riemann/Helmholtz/Clifford 核验

**1. 池检（R01–R52）**：稿文献表实测 L310–L378，无任何 Riemann/Helmholtz/Clifford 条目；全文仅 L62 正文一处提及 Riemannian。唯一可挂引痕迹：**R01（Walter 1999）二手提及**——walter99.json 实文："the geometry of space was subject in principle to empirical determination, just as Helmholtz and other physicists had claimed"（scottwalter.free.fr/papers/1999-symbuniv-walter.html，捕获于池）。→ 需新题录。

**2. 新题录（沿附加编号惯例；建议组头："Pool R53–R54 (adjunct to §2; added at the R2 literature-verification pass, 2026-09-17)"）**：

- **R53. B. Riemann, "On the Hypotheses which lie at the Bases of Geometry"（任职讲演；英译 W.K. Clifford, Nature, Vol. VIII, Nos. 183, 184, pp. 14–17, 36–37；D.R. Wilkins 转录）**。全文一手核验：riemann_emis.json（https://www.emis.de/classics/Riemann/WKCGeom.tex，HTTP 200，捕获于池）。实文直引（支撑"物理所指"方向）：
  - "the properties which distinguish space from other conceivable triply extended magnitudes are **only to be deduced from experience**"
  - "These matters of fact are—like all matters of fact—not necessary, but only of **empirical certainty**; they are hypotheses."
  - "it then results from **astronomical measurements** that it cannot be different from zero"
  - "we must seek the gound [原文如此] of its metric relations outside it, in **binding forces** which act upon it."
  - "This leads us into the domain of another science, of **physic**, into which the object of this work does not allow us to go to-day."
  - 德文原版定位：riemann_tcd.json（maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Riemann/Geom/，题名 "Ueber die Hypothesen, welche der Geometrie zu Grunde liegen." 已核，正文未捕获）。
  - 年份 caveat：EMIS 捕获件内**未标注** 1854（讲演年）与 1873（Nature 卷年）；"Riemann 1854" 现仅见于 RB-2 评述文字（REVIEW_RB2.md），camera-ready 补核。
- **R54. W.K. Clifford, "On the Space-Theory of Matter"**。全文一手核验：ws_clifford.json（en.wikisource.org/wiki/On_the_Space-Theory_of_Matter，索引页 Index:On the Space-Theory of Matter.djvu，页面归类 "1876 works"）。实文直引：
  - "I wish here to indicate a manner in which these speculations may be applied to the investigation of physical phenomena. I hold in fact (1) That small portions of space are in fact of a nature analogous to little hills on a surface which is on the average flat; namely, that the ordinary laws of geometry are not valid in them. (2) That this property of being curved or distorted is continually being passed on from one portion of space to another after the manner of a wave. (3) That this variation of the curvature of space is what really happens in that phenomenon which we call the motion of matter, whether ponderable or etherial. (4) That in the physical world nothing else takes place but this variation, subject (possibly) to the law of continuity."
  - 年份 caveat：捕获件仅见 "1876 works" 分类与文末无刊出信息，"read 21 Feb 1870 / Proc. Camb. Phil. Soc. 2 (1876)" 细目未捕获，camera-ready 补核。
