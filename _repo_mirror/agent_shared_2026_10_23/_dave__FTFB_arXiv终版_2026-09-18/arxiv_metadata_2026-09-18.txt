Abstract (verbatim from the manuscript's Abstract section; single paragraph):
------------------------------------------------
Fiction is not evidence, but it is a recurrent, checkable input to the exact sciences: four channels — formal, controlled, quantitative, narrative — separate under three checks (temporal priority, dispensability of the fictional input, operative residue), and whether machine worldviews open the reverse channel stands or falls on the same falsifiability criteria we specify. This position paper makes that sentence operational. It separates the four channels, each anchored in cases: non-Euclidean geometry as formal language prior to physical reference; thought experiments as controlled fiction under a translatability criterion; entropy as an abstract quantity that entered engineering by formal analogy and re-entered physics carrying a measurable price; and narrative fiction that supplies vocabulary and sets agendas, from a wormhole question to the singularity. A mapping (M1–M5) pairs the hardness criteria of hard science fiction with the falsifiability criteria of AI research claims in a constructed structural parallel — criteria with shared failure modes, one row an import from the pre-registration literature, declared as such — and a four-stage pipeline — setting, formalization, pre-registration, experiment — ports the mapping into a worked method, demonstrated on one artifact at the stated power; one engineering-grade, mid-strength instance is examined with its negative results disclosed in full. The reverse channel is not asserted; an adjudication agenda states what would count as feedback and how it would die. Every claim is stated to be checkable by a third party; for the engineering record, review-time availability runs through a confidential channel offered to the editors, public availability deferred to camera-ready, and the instance's temporal anchor is an author-attested freeze, recorded as provisional (§7.4).
