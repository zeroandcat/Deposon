## [51] ERC-8004 — 年份/ host 两处误写；正文 staking 表述不属实，须改写

- 现稿：ERC-8004: Trustless Agents. Ethereum RFC draft, **2026 (ethereum-magicians.org)**.
- 核验（eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004）：页头 "ERC-8004: Trustless Agents **[DRAFT]**"；作者 **Marco De Rossi, Davide Crapis, Jordan Ellis, Erik Reppel**；**Created 2025-08-13**（年份 2026 错）；规范页在 **eips.ethereum.org**（ethereum-magicians.org 仅为 Discussion Link，host 误写）。
- **建议修正文本**：
  [51] ERC-8004: Trustless Agents. Ethereum Improvement Proposal draft, created 2025-08-13. https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004. (Engineering-standard draft; non-peer-reviewed; labeled as such.)

**内容核验（任务④）**：
- "identity, reputation, and validation registries" ✅ **属实**——Specification 三节逐字对应：Identity Registry / Reputation Registry / Validation Registry。
- "staking-based validation left to composing protocols" ❌ **EIP 原文无此表述**（全文 0 处 composing/composing protocols）。可溯源的最近原文有三处：
  1. 摘要："Developers can choose from different trust models: reputation systems using client feedback, **validation via stake-secured re-execution**, zkML proofs, or TEE oracles."（stake-secured re-execution 是可选信任模型**之一**，非"留给组合协议"）；
  2. Validation Registry 节："**Generic hooks** for requesting and recording independent validators checks (e.g. **stakers re-running the job**, zkML verifiers, TEE oracles, trusted judges)."；
  3. "**Payments are orthogonal to this protocol and not covered here.**"（原文说的是 payments 出范围，不是 staking）。
- 正文 §7.2（"with staking-based validation left to composing protocols [51]"）与 §7.3 同语两处均须改写。建议表述：
  "an engineering-standard draft specifying agent identity, reputation, and validation registries, with stake-secured re-execution listed among its selectable trust models and payment mechanics declared out of scope [51]"

**簇级引用拆分判断（任务④）**：EIP-8004 全文 **0 处** deposit/escrow/bond/collateral（检索计数为证）——它不提供 bond/settlement 原语。四处簇引中：
- L4① "① commitment object: on-chain bonds attached to agent actions and side effects **[48–51]**"→**建议拆为 [48–50]**：[51] 无 commitment object（bond）语义；
- 表3 "on-chain bonds for side effects **[48–51]**"→**建议拆为 [48–50]**（同理）；
- OP3 "attach bond-settlement primitives **[48–51]**"→**建议拆为 [48–50]**；
- §9.2c "on-chain agent governance **[48–51]**, whose bonds escrow…"→指称词为 governance 时四条可合引，但 "whose bonds" 从句只覆盖 [48–50]；保守做法同样拆分。
- §7.2（已拆 [48–50]＋[51]）、§7.3（逐条）不受影响，可作为改写范式。
