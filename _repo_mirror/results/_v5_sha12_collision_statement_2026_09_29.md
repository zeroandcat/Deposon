> **Anchor caliber.** The anchors reported here are **twelve-character hexadecimal prefixes of the full SHA-256 hash** (`sha256(bytes)[:12]`, 48 bit, lowercase) — a **short human-readable identifier**, not a distinct algorithm. At the artifact count used in this paper (n ＝ 5), the birthday-bound probability of a random 48-bit collision is ≈ 3.6 × 10⁻¹⁴; this bound **does not cover adversarially constructed collisions**. The anchors therefore serve as **artifact identifiers only and are not offered as tamper-evident evidence**; integrity verification requires the **full SHA-256** value, available in the corresponding SPEC / frozen artifact.
