## §1 B1: model fingerprinting(3 URL)

1. **arXiv:2501.18712** — *Invisible Traces: Using Hybrid Fingerprinting to identify underlying LLMs in GenAI Apps* (Bhardwaj & Mishra, 2025-02)
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18712
   静态+动态混合指纹框架; 发现"仅观察 generic prompt 输出即可识别底层 model family"——与 B1 锚的"9 model T/R/A 0/1 bit 差异稳定"同向
2. **arXiv:2407.10887** — *Hey, That's My Model! Introducing Chain & Hash, An LLM Fingerprinting Technique* (Russinovich & Salem, Microsoft, 2024-07, v3 2025-06)
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10887
   密码学绑定 fingerprint prompt↔response("chain and hash"), 不可伪造——**与 P-D runs/ 哈希链思路同构**, 可作 P-F B1 的方法学参照
3. **arXiv:2509.25448** — *LLMPrint: Fingerprinting LLMs via Prompt Injection* (Duke+Ant Group, 2025-09)
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25448
   利用 prompt 注入构造指纹, 5 base model × ~700 变体验证, FP≈0

(备选: arXiv:2505.12682 RAFP rare-region 指纹; arXiv:2509.26404 SeedPrints)

## §5 B5: CoT 透明审计(3 URL, 全部直接支撑 OBSERVED_WITH_QUALIFIER)

1. **arXiv:2507.11473** — *Chain of Thought Monitorability: A New and Fragile Opportunity for AI Safety*(2025-07, **40+ 作者横跨 OpenAI/DeepMind/Anthropic/Meta/UK AISI/METR/Apollo 等**, 含 Ilya Sutskever/Hinton/Schulman 专家背书)
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11473
   核心结论: CoT 监控**有前景但脆弱**("monitorability may be fragile"), 建议前沿实验室把"对 CoT 可监控性的影响"纳入开发决策——**B5 QUALIFIER(事后合理化脆弱性)的权威佐证**
2. **arXiv:2511.08525** — *Investigating CoT Monitorability in Large Reasoning Models*(KAUST 等, 2025-11)
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08525
   首个系统研究: verbalization 忠实性 + monitor 可靠性双维度; 指出 CoT 干预(效率优化)会损害监控效果
3. **arXiv:2510.27338** — *Reasoning Models Sometimes Output Illegible Chains of Thought*(2025-10)
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27338
   14 个推理模型实测: outcome-based RL 自然导致 CoT 不可读(除 Claude 外), 强制只用可读部分时准确率掉 53%——**"CoT 公开 ≠ CoT 可审计"的直接证据**, 强化 B5 的 QUALIFIER
